COMMENT 2:
"The authors should clearly state which parts of the adaptive multi-rate 
cosimulation approach is really their original contribution, notably 
against references 19 and 20."

RESPONSE:
We thank the reviewer for highlighting the need for clearer attribution. We 
have added a dedicated subsection explicitly delineating our original 
contributions across four distinct axes against Gomes et al. [19] and 
Schweiger et al. [20].

CHANGES MADE:
- Added Section 2.3 ("Delineation of Original Contributions Against Prior 
  Co-Simulation Frameworks", lines 161–168), delineating:
  1. Continuous-Discrete Cross-Domain Coupling: Gomes et al. address 
     continuous DAE-to-DAE coupling, whereas our work solves continuous ODE 
     propagation coupled to discrete DEVS network queuing.
  2. Predictive Look-Ahead vs. Reactive Rollback: Prior FMI tools rely on 
     costly ODE state rollbacks upon event detection; our method proactively 
     contracts the coupling step ahead of occultations/ModCod thresholds, 
     eliminating event error without rollbacks.
  3. Domain-Specific Multi-Rate Regimes with Event Guards: We establish a 
     domain-specific operational hierarchy (blackout, cruise, nominal, 
     limb-grazing) with micro-event guards.
  4. Relativistic Spacetime Limits: We formulate the theoretical simultaneity 
     and proper-time bounds absent from prior co-simulation literature.
- Revised Section 1.4 to explicitly frame contributions against [19] and [20].
