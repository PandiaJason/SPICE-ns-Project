COMMENT 2:
"The authors should clearly state which parts of the adaptive multi-rate 
cosimulation approach is really their original contribution, notably 
against references 19 [Gomes et al.] and 20 [Schweiger et al.]."

RESPONSE:
We thank the reviewer for highlighting the need for clearer attribution. We 
added Section 1.3 explicitly delineating our original contributions against 
Gomes et al. [19] and Schweiger et al. [20] across four distinct axes:

1. Continuous-Discrete Cross-Domain Coupling: Prior frameworks focus on 
   continuous DAE-to-DAE coupling (e.g., automotive/power systems). We solve 
   the problem of coupling continuous astrodynamics ODE integrators to 
   discrete-event (DEVS) network queues across CCSDS ModCod switching thresholds.
2. Predictive Look-Ahead vs. Reactive Rollback: Standard co-simulation master 
   algorithms surveyed in [20] handle event zero-crossings via costly ODE state 
   rollbacks. Our engine proactively contracts coupling intervals ahead of 
   occultations and SNR gradients, eliminating event delay without rollback 
   computation.
3. Domain-Specific Multi-Rate Regimes with Event Guards: We formulate an 
   astrodynamics-specific step hierarchy (1 s micro-stepping, 600 s coasting, 
   3600 s blackout) paired with predictive boundary event guards that prevent 
   boundary overshoot and step chattering.
4. Relativistic Spacetime Limits: We formally derive the theoretical 
   simultaneity and proper-time drift bounds absent from terrestrial 
   co-simulation literature.

CHANGES MADE:
- Added Section 1.3 ("Scope, Original Contributions, and Relation to Prior Work") 
  clearly articulating these four differentiating axes.
- Revised Table 1 to accurately position the toolchain, removing the previous 
  statement that ns-3 assumes zero propagation delay and clarifying that ns-3 
  lacks relativistic frame transformations and dynamic SPICE ephemeris look-ahead.
