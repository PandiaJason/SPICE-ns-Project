Dear Dr. Ellison,

Please find enclosed our research manuscript titled \textbf{``Defying the Speed of Light: How Predictive Temporal AI Architectures Sustain Social Presence and Relational Cohesion in Deep Space Communication,''} which we are submitting for consideration as an original research article for publication in the \textit{Journal of Computer-Mediated Communication}.

This study addresses a critical operational and psychological bottleneck in interplanetary human collaboration: the severe round-trip light-time delay ($8$ to $45$ minutes at Mars distances) that disrupts conversational grounding and isolates crews. Traditional space systems isolate the crew in an ``autonomy bubble,'' leading to psychological reactance and relational friction with Ground Control. We propose the \textbf{Predictive Temporal Bridge (PTB)}, an artificial intelligence-mediated communication (AI-MC) interface that reconstructs a virtual present using dual-AI state integration, thereby sustaining social presence and shared situational awareness (SA).

We evaluate this framework using a high-fidelity interactive simulation of a critical Mars orbital insertion burn anomaly. We trace the subjective and objective effects on communication dynamics using four key indicators: the Operator Presence Fidelity Index (OPFI), the Operator Cognitive Load Index (OCLI), the Effective Command Latency (ECL), and the State Synchronization Accuracy (SSA).
